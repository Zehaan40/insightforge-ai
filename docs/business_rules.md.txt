# InsightForge AI — Business Rules

## 1. Order Status

The `orders.status` column contains three statuses:

* `completed`
* `returned`
* `canceled`

### Rules

* **Completed orders** → included in Net Revenue and Profit calculations.
* **Returned orders** → excluded from Net Revenue and Profit calculations, but may be analyzed separately as a return metric.
* **Canceled orders** → excluded from Revenue and Profit calculations.

---

## 2. Revenue

InsightForge AI will use **Net Revenue as the default business-analysis metric**.

### Net Revenue

Only completed orders are included.

```text
Revenue = (quantity × unit_price) − discount_amount
```

Therefore:

```text
Net Revenue = SUM((quantity × unit_price) − discount_amount)
```

for completed orders only.

### Gross Revenue

Gross Revenue may be calculated separately as the revenue associated with completed and returned orders before excluding returned orders.

Because the dataset does not appear to contain an actual refund amount, Gross Revenue and Net Revenue should **not** be treated as a complete accounting reconciliation.

---

## 3. Product Cost and Profit

Product cost is calculated using the product's `unit_cost`.

```text
Product Cost = quantity × unit_cost
```

Profit is calculated only for completed orders:

```text
Profit = Revenue − Product Cost
```

where:

```text
Revenue = (quantity × unit_price) − discount_amount
```

---

## 4. Profit Margin

Profit Margin is calculated as:

```text
Profit Margin (%) = (Profit / Revenue) × 100
```

Profit Margin should only be calculated when Revenue is greater than zero.

---

## 5. Discounts

There are two different discount-related fields:

* `discount_code` → order-level label
* `discount_amount` → item-level monetary discount

### Rules

* `discount_amount` is the actual monetary discount used in Revenue calculations.
* `discount_code` is used only for grouping and analysis.
* `discount_code` must not be used as a monetary discount value.

Therefore:

```text
Revenue = (quantity × unit_price) − discount_amount
```

The project should be careful when analyzing discount codes because one order can contain multiple order items.

---

## 6. Average Order Value (AOV)

AOV must be calculated at the **order level**, not the order-item level.

This prevents orders containing multiple products from being counted multiple times.

```text
AOV = Net Revenue / COUNT(DISTINCT order_id)
```

Only completed orders are included in the default AOV calculation.

---

## 7. Data Relationships

The dataset contains four main tables:

* `orders`
* `order_items`
* `products`
* `customers`

Important relationships:

```text
orders.order_id
        ↓
order_items.order_id

order_items.product_id
        ↓
products.product_id

orders.customer_id
        ↓
customers.customer_id
```

An order can contain multiple order items.

Therefore, queries involving revenue, profit, products, or categories must account for the one-to-many relationship between `orders` and `order_items`.

---

## 8. Evidence-Based AI Insights

InsightForge AI must follow this principle:

**DATA → CALCULATION → EVIDENCE → AI EXPLANATION**

The LLM must not invent business explanations that are unsupported by the data.

For example, if monthly revenue decreases, the system should first calculate the change and identify supporting evidence such as category, product, customer, or order-level changes.

The AI should clearly distinguish between:

* **Observed facts** — directly supported by the data.
* **Calculated metrics** — derived using defined formulas.
* **AI interpretation** — explanation based on the calculated evidence.
* **Recommendations** — possible actions based on the evidence.

---

## 9. Default Analysis Principle

Unless a specific analysis requires otherwise, InsightForge AI should use:

* Completed orders
* Net Revenue
* Calculated Profit
* Calculated Profit Margin
* Order-level AOV

These rules define the default business logic for Version 1 of the project.
